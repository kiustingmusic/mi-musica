ABOUT ME

The story of Kiustin G

Kiustin G is a doctor, artist, songwriter, writer and Cuban creator based in the United States.

Graduating as a physician in Cuba in 1996, he went on to build an extensive professional career spanning approximately 27 years, working in different areas of medical care.

Medicine has been an essential part of his life, but creativity has always traveled alongside it. Music, writing and audiovisual creation became different ways to express ideas, emotions and experiences.

After moving to the United States, Kiustin G continued his path in healthcare while developing a new stage of his artistic and creative work.

Today, his projects bring together music, books, technology and audiovisual production under one identity: Kiustin G.

His philosophy can be expressed in one simple idea: a life dedicated to healing, creating and leaving a mark.
