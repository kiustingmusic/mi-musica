Digital Purchases

Last updated: September 2026

1. Digital products

Some products available through kiusting.com may be offered in digital format, including PDF ebooks.

2. Payment processing

Purchases and payments may be processed through external e-commerce platforms and payment providers.

Kiusting.com does not directly store full credit-card or other financial information used during payment.

3. Delivery

Digital products are delivered electronically through the corresponding sales platform after payment is confirmed.

4. Personal use

Purchase of a digital product grants the buyer a license for personal use. Resale, redistribution, public posting, commercial duplication or unauthorized distribution of the file is not permitted.

5. Refunds

Because digital products are delivered electronically, refund requests are evaluated according to the circumstances of the purchase, the rules of the payment platform used and any rights available under applicable law.

6. Problems with a purchase

If a technical problem occurs with a download or delivery, buyers may use the support options provided by the platform through which the purchase was made.
