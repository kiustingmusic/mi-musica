How to order a custom song

A song written from your story is unlike any other gift. Here is everything you need to know before ordering yours: the process, the details that matter, and the mistakes worth avoiding.

Which occasions work

The orders that move people most are usually born from concrete moments. Birthdays top the list — Kiustin has written songs for children, mothers, friends, and even for a sanitation worker in his neighborhood who became a beloved character of his digital town. Next come tributes to Mom and to loved ones who are gone, where the song becomes a way to say out loud what was never said. Anniversaries also work beautifully, along with emigration farewells, reconciliations, a baby's birth, a business opening, and quinceañeras.

The practical rule: if there is a story and an emotion, there is a song. If you only want "something pretty and generic," you are better off listening to the catalog — custom songs are worth it precisely because they are one of a kind.

The process, step by step

Step 1 — Tell everything on WhatsApp. Write to Kiustin directly (no middlemen) with the order's details: who it is for, the occasion, the date you need it, and above all, the details. The Custom Songs page has the button that opens the conversation with the first message already drafted.

Step 2 — Everything is confirmed before a single note is written. Price, musical style (ballad, salsa, reparto, bolero, corrido…), tone (joyful, emotional, humorous), and delivery date are clear from the start. If something in your story must not be mentioned — a name, a delicate topic — say it here, not later.

Step 3 — Kiustin writes and records. The lyrics are built from your details, with the names, places, and anecdotes that make the person recognize themselves in the first ten seconds. Then the full song is recorded.

Step 4 — You receive and share. You receive your song in a lightweight file, ready to send on WhatsApp and post on Facebook or TikTok. If you ordered the lyric video, you receive that video ready to upload as well.

What you receive and what it costs

There are three formats, with public prices and no surprises: the audio song for $15 — original lyrics written for your story, recorded and ready to share; the song with lyric video for $25 — the same song with its lyric video, the format that works best for posting and gifting on social media; and the special order for $50 — custom production for big occasions, with several versions or on a rush schedule. What you pay for is the original creation: a song that exists for no one else.

The details that make an order great

The difference between a decent song and one that makes the whole family cry is in the details you send. These work especially well: the person's full name and their nickname of affection; the town or place that defines them; one or two concrete anecdotes (not "she is a very good person," but "she sold sweets on the corner to raise us"); their typical phrases or sayings; and what you want them to feel when they hear it. The more specific you are, the more the person recognizes themselves in the lyrics — that "it's talking about me!" moment is what turns a gift into a memory.

And one golden tip: order with time. Although Kiustin works fast, big dates — Mother's Day, Christmas, New Year's — concentrate many orders, and a well-told story deserves its hours.
