PRACTICAL HEALTH · SYMPTOMS

🤕 Headache

Headaches are common and can have many causes. The pattern, intensity and accompanying symptoms help determine when medical evaluation is needed.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Whether it started suddenly or gradually.
  • Where the pain is located and how intense it is.
  • Fever, stiff neck, nausea or vomiting.
  • Vision changes, weakness, numbness, confusion or trouble speaking.
  • Recent head injury.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Tension-type headache.
  • Migraine.
  • Viral illnesses and fever.
  • Dehydration or lack of sleep.
  • Other medical conditions that require evaluation.

What can you do initially?

  • Rest in a quiet environment if appropriate.
  • Drink fluids if you are able and do not have a fluid restriction.
  • Pay attention to new or unusual symptoms.
  • Seek medical advice for a new, persistent or worsening headache.

🚨 Warning signs

  • A sudden, extremely severe headache.
  • Weakness, numbness, confusion, seizure or trouble speaking.
  • Stiff neck with fever or rash.
  • Headache after a significant head injury.
  • New severe headache with major vision changes.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
