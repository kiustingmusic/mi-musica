🤢 Nausea and vomiting

Nausea and vomiting can accompany many illnesses. Hydration and warning signs are especially important.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • How often vomiting occurs and how long it has lasted.
  • Ability to keep fluids down.
  • Blood, coffee-ground material or green vomit.
  • Severe abdominal pain or severe headache.
  • Signs of dehydration such as little urine, dark urine, dry mouth or dizziness.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Gastrointestinal infections.
  • Food-related illness.
  • Migraine.
  • Medication effects.
  • Many digestive, neurologic, metabolic and other causes.

What can you do initially?

  • Take small amounts of fluid frequently if tolerated.
  • Monitor urine output and dehydration symptoms.
  • Seek medical assessment if vomiting persists or fluids cannot be kept down.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Blood or coffee-ground material in vomit.
  • Severe abdominal pain or a new severe headache.
  • Confusion, high fever with stiff neck or chest pain.
  • Significant dehydration.
  • Green vomit or rapid deterioration.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
