PRACTICAL HEALTH · SYMPTOMS

🩹 Skin spots or rash

Skin spots and rashes have many possible causes. Appearance alone is often not enough to determine the diagnosis.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • When the change appeared and whether it is spreading.
  • Itching, pain, warmth, blisters or drainage.
  • Fever or feeling significantly unwell.
  • New medicines, foods, products or exposures.
  • Swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Irritation or contact dermatitis.
  • Allergic reactions.
  • Infections.
  • Eczema and other inflammatory skin conditions.
  • Many other dermatologic or systemic conditions.

What can you do initially?

  • Avoid newly introduced products that clearly irritate the area.
  • Do not scratch or manipulate lesions.
  • Photograph changes over time if useful for medical evaluation.
  • Seek assessment for persistent, painful, spreading or unexplained lesions.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Rash with difficulty breathing or throat tightness.
  • Swelling of the lips, tongue, mouth or throat.
  • Widespread rash with fever or severe illness.
  • Rapidly spreading painful skin changes or significant blistering.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
