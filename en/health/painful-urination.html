🚽 Painful urination

Pain or burning with urination is called dysuria. Urinary tract infection is one possible cause, but it is not the only one.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Urinary frequency or urgency.
  • Cloudy, strong-smelling or bloody urine.
  • Fever or chills.
  • Pain in the back, side, pelvis or lower abdomen.
  • Genital discharge or other new symptoms.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Urinary tract infection.
  • Inflammation of the bladder or urethra.
  • Some sexually transmitted infections.
  • Prostate or vaginal conditions.
  • Stones or chemical irritation.

What can you do initially?

  • Stay appropriately hydrated unless you have a fluid restriction.
  • Do not use leftover antibiotics.
  • Seek evaluation for persistent symptoms.
  • During pregnancy, contact your healthcare team about urinary symptoms.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Fever with back or side pain.
  • Chills with nausea or vomiting.
  • Visible blood in the urine.
  • Difficulty or inability to urinate.
  • Marked worsening or severe illness.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
