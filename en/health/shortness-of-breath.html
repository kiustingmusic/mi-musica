PRACTICAL HEALTH · SYMPTOMS

🫁 Shortness of breath

Shortness of breath can occur for many reasons. Sudden or severe breathing difficulty may represent a medical emergency.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Whether it began suddenly or gradually.
  • Whether it occurs at rest or with activity.
  • Chest pain, wheezing or fever.
  • Blue or gray lips or skin.
  • Leg swelling, fainting or confusion.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Respiratory infections.
  • Asthma and other lung diseases.
  • Heart conditions.
  • Anemia and other systemic conditions.
  • Anxiety can cause breathlessness, but dangerous physical causes must not be overlooked.

What can you do initially?

  • Stop strenuous activity and sit upright if that helps breathing.
  • Use prescribed rescue medication only as directed for a known condition.
  • Seek assessment for new, unexplained or worsening breathlessness.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Severe or sudden difficulty breathing.
  • Breathlessness with chest pain, fainting or confusion.
  • Blue or gray lips or skin.
  • Rapid deterioration or inability to speak normally because of breathlessness.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
