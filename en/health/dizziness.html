💫 Dizziness

Dizziness can describe lightheadedness, imbalance or a spinning sensation called vertigo. The exact sensation and associated symptoms are important.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Whether you feel faint, unsteady or as if the room is spinning.
  • Whether it occurs when standing up or changing position.
  • Hearing changes, nausea or vomiting.
  • Chest pain, palpitations or shortness of breath.
  • Weakness, numbness, vision changes or trouble speaking.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Inner-ear disorders.
  • Dehydration or a drop in blood pressure.
  • Medication effects.
  • Migraine.
  • Other cardiovascular, neurologic or metabolic causes.

What can you do initially?

  • Sit or lie down if you feel you may fall.
  • Stand up slowly.
  • Drink fluids when appropriate and if you do not have a fluid restriction.
  • Avoid driving while significantly dizzy.
  • Seek evaluation if symptoms are new, recurrent, severe or persistent.

🚨 Warning signs

  • New severe dizziness with chest pain or difficulty breathing.
  • Fainting or seizure.
  • Sudden weakness, numbness or trouble speaking.
  • Sudden major vision or hearing change.
  • Severe headache or persistent vomiting.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
