🌡️ Fever

Fever is a temporary rise in body temperature and is often part of the body's response to an infection. Age and accompanying symptoms matter.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • The measured temperature and how it was taken.
  • How long the fever has lasted.
  • Breathing difficulty, severe headache, rash or stiff neck.
  • Hydration and urine output.
  • Age, pregnancy, immune status and important medical conditions.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Viral infections.
  • Bacterial infections.
  • Some inflammatory conditions.
  • Heat-related illness.
  • Certain medicines or other medical causes.

What can you do initially?

  • Drink adequate fluids when appropriate.
  • Rest and monitor symptoms.
  • Do not use antibiotics unless prescribed for a bacterial infection.
  • Seek medical advice when fever is persistent, concerning or accompanied by significant symptoms.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Difficulty breathing, confusion or seizure.
  • Severe headache with stiff neck.
  • Signs of severe dehydration.
  • A concerning widespread rash with significant illness.
  • Important: an infant younger than 3 months with a temperature of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher needs prompt medical assessment.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
