PRACTICAL HEALTH · SAFE USE

💊 Medicines and supplements

Educational information for using medicines safely: what they are for, common side effects, precautions and the golden rules that prevent harm. This page never recommends or doses a product — that is always your doctor's and pharmacist's job.

The golden rules of safe use

📋

Know what you take

Keep an updated list of every medicine and supplement, with doses, and take it to every appointment.

⏰

Respect the schedule

Take medicines at the indicated times. Never double a dose because you forgot one without asking first.

⚠️

Watch for side effects

New symptoms after starting a medicine may be an effect of the drug: note when they began and tell your doctor.

🚫

Never self-prescribe antibiotics

Antibiotics do not cure viruses, and using them wrongly creates resistance and harm.

🌿

Supplements are products too

“Natural” does not mean harmless: vitamins and herbs can interact with your prescription medicines.

🧑‍⚕️

Ask before you mix

Alcohol and other medicines can change how a drug works. When in doubt, ask the pharmacist.
