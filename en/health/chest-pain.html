PRACTICAL HEALTH · SYMPTOMS

❤️ Chest pain

Chest pain has many possible causes, some of which are emergencies. New or unexplained chest pain should not be self-diagnosed.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Pressure, squeezing, burning, sharp or other pain.
  • Whether pain spreads to the arm, shoulder, back, neck or jaw.
  • Shortness of breath.
  • Sweating, nausea, dizziness or fainting.
  • Whether symptoms are new, severe or unexplained.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Heart-related conditions.
  • Lung conditions.
  • Digestive causes such as reflux.
  • Muscle or chest-wall pain.
  • Other causes that require clinical assessment.

What can you do initially?

  • Do not assume new chest discomfort is indigestion.
  • Stop exertion and seek appropriate medical assessment.
  • Follow an existing emergency plan only if it was previously provided by your healthcare professional.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Call 911 for new, severe or unexplained chest pain or pressure, especially with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, fainting or pain spreading to another area.
  • Do not drive yourself when emergency symptoms are present.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
