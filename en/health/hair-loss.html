PRACTICAL HEALTH · SYMPTOMS

🧑 Hair loss

Hair loss may be gradual or sudden and can have hereditary, hormonal, medical, nutritional or medication-related causes.

📚 Medical sources

What should you notice?

  • Sudden versus gradual hair loss.
  • Patchy versus generalized thinning.
  • Scalp redness, scaling, pain or scarring.
  • Recent illness, major stress, weight change or new medicines.
  • Changes in nails, skin or other symptoms.

Possible causes

These are examples, not a diagnosis. The same symptom can have many different causes.

  • Hereditary hair loss.
  • Hormonal changes.
  • Some medical conditions.
  • Certain medicines or treatments.
  • Physical or emotional stress and nutritional factors.

What can you do initially?

  • Avoid aggressive hair treatments while the cause is unclear.
  • Review new medicines with a healthcare professional rather than stopping them yourself.
  • Seek evaluation for sudden, patchy or unexplained persistent hair loss.

🚨 Warning signs

  • Rapid hair loss with significant systemic illness.
  • Painful, inflamed or scarred scalp.
  • Hair loss accompanied by other substantial unexplained symptoms.

If you believe the situation is life-threatening, call 911 in the United States.
