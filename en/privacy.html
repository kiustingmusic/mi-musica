Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. General information

Kiusting.com is the official website of Kiustin G and provides content related to educational health information, music, videos, books, cooking, applications, creative projects, culture and other informational content.

Visitor privacy is important to Kiusting.com. This policy generally explains what information may be processed when someone uses this website and how external service providers may be involved.

Kiusting.com does not currently require visitors to create user accounts and does not directly request personal information through registration forms.

However, certain technical, hosting, security, traffic measurement, external-content or advertising providers may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, access date and time, and other identifiers necessary to provide their services.

The specific use of these technologies may depend on the service involved, the visitor's location and available consent preferences.

Kiusting.com may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to display advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to Kiusting.com or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Additional information about how Google uses data when its partners' sites or apps use Google services is available at How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps .

5. Consent and international visitors

Where required by applicable law and advertising-provider policies, Kiusting.com will use consent mechanisms to inform visitors and allow them to manage preferences regarding cookies, local storage and advertising.

This website may contain links, players or content from external services, including YouTube, social networks, music platforms, hosting services, security tools, traffic-measurement services and digital sales platforms.

Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

7. Digital purchases

Digital purchases may be processed through external e-commerce platforms and payment providers. Kiusting.com does not directly store complete payment-card information used on those platforms.

8. Health content

Health content published on Kiusting.com is provided for educational and informational purposes. The website does not require visitors to provide personal medical histories in order to access these guides.

9. User choices and rights

Depending on the visitor's location and applicable law, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection or restriction of certain data processing.

Privacy-related inquiries may be submitted through the contact section available on Kiusting.com.
